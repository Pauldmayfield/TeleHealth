## 8. Telemed IQ
- Cloud platform: desktop/mobile apps, provider-to-patient and provider-to-provider video, bi-directional FHIR EMR integration (consult requests inside EMR), client portal or 24/7 US clinical support center, analytics/benchmarking, TJC quality and physician performance management; works on own hardware, Access TeleCare carts, or both; "deployed in over 2,200 programs". REPORTED: https://accesstelecare.com/telemediq-telemedicine-platform
- Hospitals can run their own physicians, use Access's network, or hybrid (same page). The page does not describe a separately priced standalone license. The 10-K (2021) said it is offered standalone and counted "Telemed IQ/other" as ~half of 2021 consults (254,002 of 508,816). REPORTED. The 2021 count was likely inflated by non-network use; a current figure is unknown.
- Third-party listing claims 500+ hospitals/SNFs use it (marithealth listing, UNVERIFIED; conflicts with 2,200 programs because programs and facilities are different units).
- Customer story: Zoom first used in a HIPAA environment by Access TeleCare in 2015.
