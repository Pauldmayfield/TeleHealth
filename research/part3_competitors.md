## 2. Insourcing / hub cases
- Dartmouth Health (Aug 2026): ending Tele-ICU and Tele-ED, serving 13 rural NH/VT hospitals; part of ~20 Connected Care services launched 2012; calls them "simply unsustainable" at current scale without external support; $63.5M operating deficit in May; no cost figure or end date. REPORTED: https://www.concordmonitor.com/2026/08/17/dartmouth-health-telehealth-programs-for-icu-emergency-rooms-are-too-expensive-to-keep-going/ and https://vnews.com/2026/08/18/dartmouth-health-ends-tele-services/ . Valley News: Grace Cottage will likely move fully to Avel (already covers nights/weekends), CMO says Avel "not quite as efficient, not quite as personalized." Concord Monitor does not mention Avel. Both accurate to their pages; Avel link is one hospital only. This is a system-run hub being shut down, a demand tailwind for outside vendors (Avel named; Access TeleCare not named).
- Not found: any fetched 2025-26 case of a system insourcing teleneuro/tele-ICU from a vendor, or a handback. A Becker's piece says some vendor exits have moved services in-house but names no telestroke case (UNVERIFIED snippet): https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/innovation/things-are-moving-too-fast-health-systems-intensify-it-vendor-scrutiny/
