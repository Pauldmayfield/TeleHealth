## 3. Exit track record
Finding: PSC's own news feed (all 6 pages) shows essentially no completed exits; the firm is young (2020), and most holds are 1-5 years. Only one disclosed sale:
- Radius Health: B-FLEXION to acquire the remaining 57% from PSC affiliates and Webster Equity Partners; announced Jan 12, 2026; expected close by end Q1 2026; terms undisclosed. Type: sale to co-investor/strategic-sponsor hybrid (B-FLEXION was co-buyer in 2022). Hold ~3.4 yrs from Aug 15, 2022 take-private (https://patientsquarecapital.com/news/page/4/). Momtazee: "a good time to deliver returns for our investors and consolidate ownership". REPORTED: https://patientsquarecapital.com/b-flexion-life-sciences-to-acquire-remaining-57-interest-in-radius-health/ . PitchBook reportedly dates the exit Mar 16, 2026 (UNVERIFIED, snippet only: https://pitchbook.com/profiles/investor/438163-57).
- Hanger/Numotion (Sept 10, 2026): a merger where PSC keeps control, not an exit. One aggregator labels it an "exit" (UNVERIFIED); PSC's release is more credible.
- No IPOs, no sponsor-to-sponsor sales found. Pattern, as ESTIMATED from small sample: PSC is a buyer of public-to-private (SOC, Hanger, Radius, Patterson, Premier, Syneos) and of sponsor-owned assets (Summit from FFL/Lee, Paradigm from OMERS), so sponsors are sellers to it. Sample too small to call exit preference. Access TeleCare hold as of today: ~4.6 years (Apr 2022); a typical PE hold is 4-7 years (general knowledge, ESTIMATED), so an exit window is plausible 2026-2028. D Magazine (May 2026) discusses repositioning after "$400 million take-private" (snippet only; page 403): https://www.dmagazine.com/healthcare-business/2026/05/access-telecare-400-million-repositioning/ . Deal value ~$304M equity per SOC release summaries; "$400M" likely includes debt (ESTIMATED).
